## 🚀 Current Quarter (Q3 2026)

### Focus: Stability & Mobile Polish
- **Offline Sync V2**: Improving conflict resolution algorithms and background sync reliability.
- **PWA Enhancements**: Native-like app install banners, push notifications for habits/reminders.
- **Performance Tuning**: Reducing bundle size by 15% and optimizing the initial load time.
- **Orbit AI - Smart Tagging**: AI automatically suggests tags and folders for your unorganized Notes and Tasks.

---

## 📅 Next Quarter (Q4 2026)

### Focus: Collaboration & Integrations
- **Shared Workspaces**: Ability to invite other users to specific Workspaces (e.g., family planner, team projects).
- **Calendar Integration**: Two-way sync with Google Calendar and Apple Calendar.
- **Advanced Goals**: Sub-goals, milestone tracking, and visual progress charts.
- **Public API Beta**: Releasing a secure REST API for power users to build their own integrations.

---

## 🔮 Future Horizons (2027 & Beyond)

### Focus: Intelligence & Ecosystem
- **Orbit AI - Predictive Planning**: The AI analyzes your past performance and suggests realistic daily schedules.
- **Desktop Apps**: Native wrappers (Tauri or Electron) for macOS and Windows with deep OS integration (global shortcuts, menu bar apps).
- **Marketplace**: A hub for community-created templates (Habit trackers, Note systems).
- **End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)**: Optional zero-knowledge encryption for highly sensitive Vault notes.
