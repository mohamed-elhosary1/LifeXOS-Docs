# LifeXOS User Guide

Welcome to LifeXOS! This guide will help you navigate and utilize the application to its fullest.

## Navigating the App

The main navigation is located in the left sidebar. Click on any section to jump to that view.

### My Day
The central hub for your daily focus.
- **Purpose**: View tasks you've specifically designated for today, alongside daily habits.
- **Actions**:
  - Click the circle next to a task to complete it.
  - Drag tasks to reorder them.
  - Use "Rush Mode" for rapid execution of tasks.

### To Do
Your master task list.
- **Purpose**: Store all your tasks.
- **Actions**:
  - Add tasks using the input at the top or `QuickAdd`.
  - Click on a task to edit its details, add a due date, or move it to "My Day".

### Planned & Planner
Schedule your life.
- **Purpose**: View upcoming tasks based on their assigned dates.
- **Actions**:
  - See tasks organized by day/week.
  - Reschedule tasks by dragging them to different dates.

### Habits & Goals
Build consistency and achieve milestones.
- **Habits**: Add daily routines and click the calendar squares to mark them done.
- **Goals**: Create long-term objectives and track progress bars as you complete associated milestones.

### Study & Pomodoro
Focus tools.
- **Purpose**: Use timed sessions to work without distraction.
- **Actions**:
  - Start a Pomodoro timer.
  - Take scheduled breaks.

### Notes
Your digital scratchpad.
- **Purpose**: Jot down ideas, meeting notes, or journal entries.
- **Actions**: Create, edit, and delete text notes.

### Orbit AI Copilot
Your personal assistant.
- **Purpose**: Helps parse tasks, organize your day, and provide insights.
- **Actions**: Interact with the AI banner or copilot chat interface to ask questions or issue commands.

## General Shortcuts & Interactions
- **Drag & Drop**: Supported across Task lists. Grab the handle on a `TaskItem` to reorder.
- **Quick Add**: Use the global add button to quickly input tasks or notes from any screen.
- **Theming**: Toggle dark/light mode in the Settings page.
