## The Philosophy

Modern digital life is fragmented. We use one app for tasks, another for notes, a third for habit tracking, and yet another for goal setting. This fragmentation leads to:
- **Context Switching:** Wasted time moving between applications.
- **Data Silos:** Information in your notes doesn't easily connect to your daily tasks.
- **Overwhelm:** Managing multiple systems creates friction.

LifeXOS solves this by providing a single, elegantly designed interface where all these modules are deeply interconnected. Your daily tasks inform your long-term goals; your habits are tracked alongside your daily planner; and your notes can instantly generate actionable tasks.

## Core Modules

### 1. Workspaces
The foundation of LifeXOS. Create isolated or connected workspaces for different areas of your life (e.g., "Work," "Personal," "Side Hustle"). Everything inside LifeXOS lives within a workspace, allowing for clean organization.

### 2. Planner (MyDay & Planned)
Your daily command center. `MyDay` helps you focus on what needs to be done *today*, filtering out the noise. The `Planned` view gives you a broader perspective of upcoming deadlines and scheduled tasks.

### 3. Tasks & Habits
- **Tasks:** Manage your to-dos with an intuitive interface powered by `@dnd-kit` for seamless drag-and-drop prioritization.
- **Habits:** Build routines that stick. Visual streaks, heatmaps, and progress tracking keep you motivated.

### 4. Notes & Goals
- **Notes:** A rich Markdown editor for capturing thoughts, meeting minutes, and journal entries.
- **Goals:** Define your high-level objectives (e.g., "Learn a new language") and break them down into trackable milestones and tasks.

## The Role of Orbit AI

What truly sets LifeXOS apart is **Orbit AI**. Orbit is not just a chatbot bolted onto the side; it is deeply integrated into the OS. 
- It understands the context of your current workspace.
- It can summarize your notes, suggest daily schedules, and analyze your habit trends.
- It proactively notifies you to help you stay on track.
